I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at The University of Texas at Arlington, advised by Ashraf Aboulnaga. I work at the intersection of database systems and machine learning, building efficient and scalable data systems for AI/ML workloads. Lately that means using the database itself (SQL, query processing, statistics, incremental maintenance) to make learning over large relational data faster and cheaper.

Before my Ph.D., I spent over two years in industry as a machine learning engineer, building and deploying production systems for speech recognition, NLP and LLM-powered applications. I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Islamic University of Technology (IUT), Bangladesh.

Outside of research, I enjoy talking about data-driven systems, and I am a huge football fan. Chelsea is my club. Keep the blue flag flying high!

Interests DBMS support for ML workloads · Relational deep learning · Graph data processing · Big data mining
